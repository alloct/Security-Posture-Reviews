Assessment Methodology

{{ settings.company_name }} conducts an annual security posture assessment to provide a structured, repeatable measurement of a client's information security maturity. The assessment is workshop-led and based on responses provided by the client's IT and security stakeholders, supplemented where possible by technical evidence such as vulnerability scans.

Scope

The assessment covers eight control domains: Identity and Access Management; Endpoint Security; Network Security; Data Protection and Backup; Vulnerability and Patch Management; Security Awareness and Training; Incident Response and Business Continuity; and Compliance and Governance. Each domain contains a set of control questions that examine the presence, scope, and operational maturity of expected safeguards.

Scoring

Each control question is weighted by criticality. Questions covering high-impact controls such as multi-factor authentication, EDR coverage, and immutable backups carry higher weights. Each response is scored as fully implemented, partially implemented, or not implemented and is multiplied by the question weight to produce earned points. The overall posture score is expressed as a percentage of the maximum achievable points.

Where a Nessus vulnerability scan is supplied, the technical evidence is used to adjust the posture score. Each Critical severity finding deducts 1.5 points and each High severity finding deducts 0.5 points, capped at 15 and 10 points respectively. Medium, Low, and Informational findings are reported but do not impact the score.

Limitations

This assessment is a point-in-time review based on information made available by the client. It does not constitute a technical penetration test or a regulatory audit. Findings should be interpreted alongside the client's risk appetite, business context, and any active improvement initiatives.
